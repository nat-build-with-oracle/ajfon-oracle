## Lessons Learned

- **Privacy scrutiny belongs at draft-time, not just publish-time.** When writing any content bound for a public artifact/repo, check "whose words are these, and did they consent to this audience" while writing it, not only when asked to ship it — the check is cheaper before the content exists in two files than after.
- **On privacy-adjacent decisions, raise the bar for "clear enough instruction" before acting on an ambiguous reply.** A vague reply to a routine technical question can reasonably be resolved by best-guess-and-proceed; a vague reply to a "is it OK to expose this person's private words" question deserves either a re-ask or the more conservative reading, not just the more likely reading.
- **Peeking a target oracle's live state before sending a federation query measurably improves first-try success** — an idle oracle that just finished directly-relevant work is a much better target for a one-shot question than one mid-task on something unrelated.
